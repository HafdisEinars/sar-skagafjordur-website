Aldan is the rescue boat of **Skagfirðingasveit** in Sauðárkrókur and an important part of the team's sea rescue equipment. With Aldan, the team has stronger capacity to respond to incidents on the sea, along the coast and on the lakes and waters of Skagafjörður, where conditions can change quickly and every minute can matter.

## Built for Changing Conditions

Search and rescue work in Skagafjörður is shaped by weather, water, terrain and distance. The team has long worked to strengthen its ability to respond safely and effectively when a call comes in near the sea or on inland waters.

Before Aldan arrived, the team often had to rely on smaller equipment in situations that called for greater capacity, better safety and more reliable tools for volunteers working in demanding conditions.

## A Long Road to Aldan

The road to Aldan was not short. Skagfirðingasveit had worked for some time to improve its sea rescue equipment, and the need for a stronger boat had become clear through the team's experience and callouts.

Community support made the difference. **FISK Seafood** in Sauðárkrókur stepped in and fully funded the new rescue boat, giving the volunteers a stronger foundation for future response work.

## A Gift for the Whole Community

Aldan was formally handed over at Borgarröst 1 in Sauðárkrókur on **15 March 2025**. Volunteers, supporters and representatives from FISK Seafood gathered to mark a milestone that was about more than a boat.

It was about safety. It was about readiness. And it was about what can happen when a local company, volunteers and the wider community pull in the same direction.

## More Than Equipment

Today, Aldan is part of the trusted support system people rely on when something goes wrong. The boat strengthens the team's ability to respond around water, but the heart of the work is still the same: people, training, teamwork and the willingness to show up when help is needed.

The name Aldan means **the wave**. It is a fitting name for a rescue boat in Skagafjörður. It reminds us of the power of nature, but also of the strength that is created when volunteers and community stand together.
