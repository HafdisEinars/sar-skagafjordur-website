# International Marketing Starter Kit

This note collects campaign links, ad copy, short video scripts and a first
update-list email for small international tests. The public site stays
English-only and GoatCounter will automatically group visits that arrive with
`utm_campaign` or `utm_source` parameters.

## Suggested First Test

Run one small campaign for 3-4 weeks before increasing spend. A good first test
is to promote the online talk page with short videos and a simple "register
interest" call to action.

Review these numbers before scaling:

- visits by campaign in GoatCounter
- online-talk page visits
- contact clicks
- update-list clicks
- story-card clicks
- donate clicks

## Short Video Scripts

These scripts are written for short vertical videos. Keep them simple: one clear
idea, real rescue-team images or video, subtitles on screen, and a direct CTA.

### Short 1: What Happens After 1-1-2?

Length: 25-35 seconds

Goal: Send people to the online talk page.

Visual ideas: team vehicles, radio/phone details, rescue gear, people preparing
equipment, road or landscape shots.

Voiceover:
`In Iceland, if someone is missing, injured or in danger outdoors, the call often starts with 1-1-2.`

`Behind that call are volunteer rescue teams. People leave work, school, family dinners or sleep to respond.`

`They bring vehicles, boats, radios, ropes, search equipment and local knowledge.`

`In our online talk, we explain how Icelandic search and rescue really works.`

On-screen text:
`What happens after 1-1-2?`

`Volunteer rescue teams respond`

`Training, equipment, teamwork`

`Join the online talk`

Caption:
`Icelandic search and rescue is built on volunteers, training and local readiness. Learn how it works in our online talk.`

CTA link:
Online talks campaign link

### Short 2: A Volunteer Team, A Real Community

Length: 25-35 seconds

Goal: Build trust and curiosity.

Visual ideas: group photos, training evenings, youth team, equipment room,
coffee table, team house.

Voiceover:
`Our rescue team is made of volunteers. Some are students. Some work full time. Some have decades of experience.`

`We train, fundraise, maintain equipment and prepare for the callouts we hope will never come.`

`Skagfirðingasveit serves a small community in North Iceland, but the work can be anything but small.`

`Come behind the scenes with us in an online talk.`

On-screen text:
`A rescue team run by volunteers`

`Training every week`

`Ready when needed`

`Inside Icelandic Search and Rescue`

Caption:
`Meet the volunteer system behind Icelandic search and rescue.`

CTA link:
Online talks campaign link

### Short 3: Why Fireworks Matter

Length: 25-35 seconds

Goal: Explain fundraising and support.

Visual ideas: fireworks sale, team members working, vehicles, gear, rescue boat,
training clips.

Voiceover:
`People often ask why rescue teams in Iceland sell fireworks.`

`The short answer is: readiness costs money. Vehicles, boats, radios, protective clothing, training and maintenance all matter before the emergency happens.`

`Fundraising is part of how volunteer rescue teams stay ready.`

`If you want to understand the system, join the talk or support the team.`

On-screen text:
`Why do rescue teams sell fireworks?`

`Readiness costs money`

`Volunteers train before the callout`

`Learn more or support the team`

Caption:
`Behind every callout is preparation: training, equipment, maintenance and volunteers.`

CTA link:
Online talks campaign link or Support-focused campaign link

## First Update-List Email

Use this as the first email to people who have joined the update list.

Subject options:

- `Inside Icelandic Search and Rescue`
- `A look behind the scenes of Icelandic rescue work`
- `What happens when someone calls 1-1-2 in Iceland?`

Preview text:
`A new online talk from Skagfirðingasveit, a volunteer rescue team in North Iceland.`

Email body:

```text
Hi,

Thank you for joining the update list for Skagfirðingasveit.

We are working on a new online talk: Inside Icelandic Search and Rescue.

The talk will be a behind-the-scenes introduction to volunteer search and rescue in Iceland: how the teams are organised, how training works, what happens after a 1-1-2 call, why equipment and fundraising matter, and what visitors most often ask us about rescue work in Iceland.

It is based on the same kind of presentation we give to visiting groups in Skagafjörður, but adapted for people who want to join online.

You can register interest here:
https://sarice.is/online-talks.html

We will send more details when the first date or recorded version is ready.

Thank you for following and supporting the team.

Skagfirðingasveit
https://sarice.is
```

## Simple Launch Order

1. Post Short 1 organically on Facebook/Instagram.
2. Send the first update-list email.
3. Run a small paid test to the online talk page.
4. Review GoatCounter after the first week.
5. Keep the best-performing hook and make the next two short videos.

## Practical Targeting Ideas

- People interested in Iceland travel, North Iceland, hiking, adventure travel,
  winter travel and outdoor safety.
- People interested in volunteering, rescue work, emergency services and
  community support.
- Start with one or two countries at a time so the results are easier to read.
