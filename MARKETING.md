# International marketing starter kit

This note collects simple campaign links and ad copy for small international tests.
The public site stays English-only and GoatCounter will automatically group visits
that arrive with `utm_campaign` or `utm_source` parameters.

## Campaign links

- General awareness:
  `https://sarice.is/?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=international_awareness`
- Support-focused:
  `https://sarice.is/?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=support_rescue_team#support`
- Stories-focused:
  `https://sarice.is/stories.html?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=rescue_stories`
- Online talks:
  `https://sarice.is/online-talks.html?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=online_talks`
- Search ads / Iceland rescue team:
  `https://sarice.is/about.html?utm_source=google&utm_medium=paid_search&utm_campaign=iceland_rescue_team`

## Suggested first test

Run one small campaign for 3-4 weeks before increasing spend. Send people to either
the Stories page or the Support section, then review:

- visits by campaign in GoatCounter
- story-card clicks
- donate clicks
- contact clicks
- update-list clicks

## Ad copy options

Headline: `Volunteer Search and Rescue in North Iceland`

Text: `Meet Skagfirðingasveit, a volunteer rescue team serving Skagafjörður through search, rescue, training and community readiness.`

## Short video ideas

- Hook: `What happens in Iceland when someone calls 1-1-2?`
  CTA: `Join the online talk`
- Hook: `Iceland does search and rescue differently. Here is why.`
  CTA: `Meet the volunteers`
- Hook: `This rescue team is run by volunteers aged 18 to 85.`
  CTA: `Learn how it works`
- Hook: `Why do Icelandic rescue teams sell fireworks?`
  CTA: `Support the team`
- Hook: `A small town rescue team, one boat, many callouts.`
  CTA: `Watch the full talk`
- Hook: `What tourists usually ask us about rescue work in Iceland.`
  CTA: `Register interest`

## Practical targeting ideas

- People interested in Iceland travel, North Iceland, hiking, adventure travel, and outdoor safety.
- People interested in volunteering, rescue work, emergency services, and community support.
- Start with one or two countries at a time so the results are easier to read.
