Help keep volunteers ready for the next callout

Skagfirðingasveit is powered by volunteers. Donations help with training, rescue equipment, communications, protective gear, and the ongoing care of vehicles and the rescue boat Aldan.

  • Training Regular practice keeps volunteers prepared for difficult conditions.
  • Equipment Reliable tools, protective gear, and communications make every response safer.
  • Water response Aldan strengthens the team's ability to respond around rivers, harbours, and coastlines.
