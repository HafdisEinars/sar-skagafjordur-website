About Us

A volunteer rescue team built on commitment and community

Skagfirdingasveit is part of Iceland’s volunteer search and rescue tradition. Our work is rooted in preparedness, teamwork, training, and service.

We respond when people need help, support safety and prevention efforts, and welcome opportunities to share our work with visitors, schools, and groups.

What We Do

Search, rescue, education, and readiness

Our volunteers train regularly and take part in a wide range of work, from response and field operations to public safety outreach and community support.

Donations and local support make this work possible. Every contribution helps us maintain equipment, training, and readiness.
