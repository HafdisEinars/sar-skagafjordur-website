For visitors, schools and groups

A human look at rescue work in Iceland

This talk is based on the presentation we use with international visitors who come to meet the team in Sauðárkrókur. The slides are only the frame; the real value is in the stories, questions, explanations, and local detail.

It introduces how Iceland's volunteer rescue system works, what happens when 1-1-2 calls, how teams train, how equipment is maintained, and why community support matters.

The format can work as a live online talk with Q&A, a private group presentation, or later as a recorded on-demand session.

What the talk covers

From the national system to the next callout

The talk follows the same path visitors often ask about in person: who the rescue teams are, how they prepare, and what happens when the phone rings.

  1. The Icelandic rescue system How rescue teams, accident prevention groups, and Landsbjörg fit together.
  2. Life inside a local rescue team Volunteers, ages, skills, equipment, the rescue house, and everyday readiness.
  3. Training and youth work Weekly practices, weekend courses, youth team activity, and why training never stops.
  4. Fundraising and community support Fireworks, local work, donations, and the practical cost of staying ready.
  5. Callouts and real questions How 1-1-2 callouts work, how teams choose equipment, and what visitors usually want to know.
